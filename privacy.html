Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

At PlayThings®, we believe trust is earned — and that includes how we handle your information. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

What We Collect

We collect only what we need to stay in touch with you:

  • Email address — when you sign up for updates
  • Basic analytics — anonymous usage data to understand how visitors interact with our site

We don't sell your data. We don't share it with third parties for marketing. Period.

How We Use Your Information

Your email is used to:

  • Send you occasional updates about PlayThings®
  • Share relevant information about hosting opportunities
  • Respond if you reach out to us

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time — there's a link at the bottom of every message. If you'd like us to delete your information entirely, just email us at privacy@playthings.com.

Data Security

Your information is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We use Supabase for our database, which provides enterprise-grade security and compliance.

Cookies

We use minimal, essential cookies to make our site work properly. We don't use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, if appropriate, notify you via email.

Questions?

If you have questions about our privacy practices, please reach out to privacy@playthings.com. We're happy to clarify anything.
