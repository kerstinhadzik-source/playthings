## 1. BRAND STORY & PURPOSE

### The Origin

playthings was born from a simple observation: the women who build relationship-based businesses — the ones who show up with trust, credibility, and genuine connection — are almost always the ones left without a structure that protects what they've built.

They're told to "trust the process" right before the terms shift. They build something real, and then the company behind them restructures, pivots, or quietly moves around them.

playthings exists to do the opposite.

### The Purpose

playthings is the corporate structure for relationship-based businesses. We handle fulfillment, logistics, and product continuity so the women who've already done the hard work of earning trust can keep serving their customers — without disruption, dilution, or competition.

We stand behind them. Not over them.

### Who We Serve

Women who have built real relationships with real customers. Women who left (or were left by) MLM structures and decided to go independent. Women who need stable infrastructure — not another system to learn, another brand to promote, or another set of rules that might change.

## 3. BRAND VOICE & TONE

### Voice: Calm, Direct, Honest

playthings sounds like a conversation with someone who respects your intelligence. We don't use hype. We don't inflate. We don't create urgency where there isn't any.

### Tone Attributes

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm** | Approachable but not performative. We care — we don't pretend to. | "We'd love to talk. No pitch." |
| **Direct** | We say what we mean. No filler, no jargon, no corporate-speak. | "Your relationships stay yours." |
| **Confident** | We know what we are. We don't need to convince you. | "The structure does not change." |
| **Honest** | If we don't know, we say so. If something isn't for you, we say that too. | "You don't know us yet. That's intentional." |
| **Restrained** | Less is more. We earn trust through consistency, not volume. | One clear sentence > three fluffy ones. |

### Voice Do's

- Speak in short, clear sentences
- Use "you" more than "we"
- Lead with what matters to the reader, not to us
- Let silence do the work — white space, breathing room
- Be specific ("No shifting terms") not vague ("We value integrity")

### Voice Don'ts

- Never use exclamation marks in headlines or primary copy
- Never use words like: amazing, incredible, game-changer, crushing it, boss babe, hustle, grind, tribe, family (in business context), journey (unless literal)
- Never create false urgency: "limited time," "act now," "don't miss out"
- Never use emojis in primary brand communications
- Never speak for the customer: "You're going to LOVE this"
- Never disparage competitors by name

## 5. VISUAL IDENTITY

### Design Philosophy

playthings looks like it feels: calm, warm, intentional, and understated. Our visual identity is built on restraint. We use fewer elements, more white space, and let the content breathe.

The aesthetic is: **editorial, not commercial.** Think high-end lifestyle magazine, not e-commerce catalog.

### Design Principles

1. **Warmth over flash** — soft tones, organic textures, no neon or harsh contrast
2. **Space is sacred** — generous margins, breathing room between elements
3. **Typography leads** — the typeface carries the brand; imagery supports, never dominates
4. **Consistency over novelty** — every touchpoint should feel like the same quiet room
5. **Dark sections for gravity** — charcoal backgrounds signal importance and trust

## 6. TYPOGRAPHY

### Primary Typeface: Meno Display

Meno Display is used across the entire brand — headlines, body, navigation, buttons, everything. One font, multiple weights.

| Use | Weight | Size Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Headlines (H1)** | 300 (Light) | 2.4rem – 3.6rem | `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 3.6rem)` |
| **Section Headlines (H2)** | 300 (Light) | 2rem – 2.9rem | `clamp(2rem, 4vw, 2.9rem)` |
| **Subheadlines (H3)** | 300 (Light) | 1.2rem – 1.5rem | Used for emphasis blocks |
| **Body Text** | 300 (Light) | 1.05rem | Line-height: 1.8 |
| **Small Text / Labels** | 300–400 | 0.75rem – 0.9rem | Uppercase with letter-spacing for labels |
| **Buttons** | 400 (Regular) | 0.9rem – 1.05rem | Sentence case |
| **Navigation** | 300 (Light) | 0.9rem | |

### Fallback Stack

```
'Meno Display', Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif
```

### Typography Rules

- **Never use bold (600+)** for large text — the brand's lightness is the point
- **Use 400 (Regular)** sparingly — for emphasis within light-weight body copy, button labels, founder name
- **Letter-spacing:** `0.02em` on wordmark, `0.08–0.15em` on uppercase labels
- **Line-height:** 1.25 for headlines, 1.7–1.8 for body text
- **All-lowercase** for the wordmark "playthings" — always

- **Never use pure black (#000000)** for text or backgrounds. Always use `#1a1a1a` or `#42423f`.
- **Never use bright, saturated colors.** No red, blue, green, orange, or any color that breaks the warm neutral palette.

## 8. LOGO & WORDMARK

### The Wordmark

playthings uses a **text-only wordmark** — no icon, no symbol, no graphic mark. The name is the brand.

| Attribute | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Text** | `playthings` |
| **Case** | Always lowercase |
| **Typeface** | Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif (web) / Meno Display (print) |
| **Weight** | 400 (Regular) |
| **Size** | 1.85rem (desktop) / 1.4rem (mobile) |
| **Color** | White on dark backgrounds · Warm Taupe on cream backgrounds |
| **Letter-spacing** | `0.02em` |
| **Text-shadow** | `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.3)` on dark backgrounds only |

### Wordmark Rules

- **Always lowercase.** Never capitalize: not "Playthings," not "PlayThings," not "PLAYTHINGS."
- **Never add a logo mark, icon, or graphic** next to the wordmark.
- **Minimum clear space:** At least 1x the height of the "p" on all sides.
- **Never stretch, warp, rotate, or outline** the wordmark.
- **Never place the wordmark on busy photography** without a dark overlay or solid background.
- **The ® symbol** may be used in legal contexts (PlayThings®) but is omitted in brand/marketing usage.

### Wordmark Color Variants

| Context | Color |
|---|---|
| Navigation (dark bar) | White `#ffffff` |
| Footer (dark background) | White `#ffffff` |
| Light backgrounds | Warm Taupe `#9a8b80` |
| Print (single color) | Black `#1a1a1a` or Warm Taupe |

## 9. PHOTOGRAPHY & IMAGERY

### Style

- **Warm, natural lighting** — golden hour, soft window light, warm studio
- **Muted tones** — desaturated slightly, never oversaturated
- **Clean compositions** — lots of breathing room, minimal props
- **Real people, real moments** — no stock-photo smiles, no staged perfection
- **Product photography:** cream/neutral backgrounds, warm shadows, minimal styling

### Do's

- Photograph products on cream, linen, or marble surfaces
- Use warm-toned filters or presets that match the brand palette
- Show products in context (on a nightstand, in a gift box, next to candles)
- Feature real customers and reps (with permission)
- Use negative space generously

### Don'ts

- No harsh flash, fluorescent lighting, or cool blue tones
- No cluttered compositions or busy backgrounds
- No text overlays on product images (use separate graphic elements)
- No stock photos of generic "happy women" or office settings
- No explicit imagery — products are shown with taste, never vulgarity

### Image Treatment (Web)

- **Subtle grain texture** overlays the entire site (SVG noise filter at 2% opacity)
- **Pinstripe pattern** on dark sections (repeating 2px vertical lines at 2% white opacity)
- **mix-blend-mode: lighten** on hero product images to blend with dark backgrounds
- **Border-radius: 8–12px** on card images and founder photos

## 10. DIGITAL & WEB STANDARDS

### Layout

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Max content width** | 1400px (1200px for narrow sections) |
| **Side padding** | `var(--space-lg)` = 2.25rem (desktop) / 1rem (mobile) |
| **Section spacing** | `var(--space-2xl)` = 5rem between major sections |
| **Card border-radius** | 8–12px |
| **Button border-radius** | 8px (rectangular) / 100px (pill-shaped filter buttons) |

### Spacing Scale

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--space-xs` | 0.5rem (8px) | Tight gaps, tag spacing |
| `--space-sm` | 1rem (16px) | Default gap, paragraph spacing |
| `--space-md` | 1.5rem (24px) | Section internal padding |
| `--space-lg` | 2.25rem (36px) | Section side padding, major gaps |
| `--space-xl` | 3.5rem (56px) | Section top/bottom padding |
| `--space-2xl` | 5rem (80px) | Between major sections |

### Animations

| Animation | Duration | Easing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reveal on scroll** | 0.9s | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | Elements fade up 30px on viewport entry |
| **Hover transitions** | 0.3s | Same easing | Buttons, links, cards |
| **Scroll progress bar** | 0.1s | Linear | 2px bar, taupe-to-charcoal gradient |
| **Cursor glow** | 0.08 lerp | N/A | 400px radial gradient, 6% opacity, desktop only |

### Accessibility

- **Skip link** on every page
- **ARIA labels** on all interactive elements
- **Reduced motion** preference respected (animations disabled)
- **Focus-visible outlines** in warm taupe, 3px offset
- **Minimum touch targets:** 44px on mobile
- **Safe area insets** supported for notched devices
- **Color contrast:** All text meets WCAG AA against its background

### Buttons

| Type | Background | Text | Padding | Border-Radius |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary (CTA)** | Warm Taupe `#9a8b80` | White `#fff` | `1rem 3rem` | 8px |
| **Primary Hover** | Warm Taupe Hover `#ab9d92` | White | Slight translateY(-2px) | 8px |
| **Filter Pill** | Transparent (border: 1px) | Charcoal Gray | `0.6rem 1.25rem` | 100px |
| **Filter Pill Active** | Warm Taupe | White | Same | 100px |

## 11. SOCIAL MEDIA GUIDELINES

### Platform Presence

| Platform | Handle | Primary Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | @playthings | Brand storytelling, product aesthetics, rep spotlights |
| **TikTok** | @playthings | Product demos, behind-the-scenes, voice-led content |
| **Facebook** | playthings | Community, events, longer-form content |

### Content Pillars

1. **Behind the Structure** — How playthings works, what makes it different, founder perspective
2. **Product Spotlight** — Individual products shown with taste, warm styling, use-case context
3. **Rep Stories** — Amplifying the women who use the structure (their voice, not ours)
4. **Trust & Transparency** — "The rules don't change" messaging, anti-hype positioning
5. **Lifestyle** — Candles, self-care, date night — the world playthings products live in

### Visual Standards for Social

- Use the brand palette — warm taupe, cream, charcoal
- No bright colors, no neon, no harsh filters
- Text on images: Meno Display or Georgia, always lowercase for brand name
- Grid aesthetic: alternating warm/dark tones, consistent spacing
- No stock photos. Ever.

### Caption Voice

- Write like you're texting a friend who's smart and doesn't need to be sold
- Short paragraphs. One idea per line.
- End with an invitation, not a demand: "If this resonates, we'd love to hear from you."
- No hashtag spam — 3-5 relevant tags maximum

## 13. BRAND APPLICATIONS

### Website (myplaythings.com)

- Cream backgrounds with dark charcoal accent sections
- One typeface (Meno Display), light weight throughout
- Subtle grain texture overlay
- Pinstripe pattern on dark sections
- Scroll-reveal animations at 0.9s
- Cursor glow on desktop
- Scroll progress bar (2px, top of viewport)
- Mobile-optimized with safe-area support

### Email

- Plain, text-forward emails — minimal graphics
- Warm taupe accent for links
- Sign off as "Kerstin" or "Kerstin Hadzik, Founder" — never "The playthings Team"
- No headers with giant logos — the wordmark is small and quiet

### Packaging (Future)

- Cream/kraft boxes with warm taupe wordmark
- Minimal text on exterior — just the wordmark and a tagline
- Interior tissue paper in soft cream
- No bright colors, no excessive branding

### Print (Business Cards, One-Pagers)

- Cream or white stock, warm taupe ink
- Wordmark only — no graphic logo
- Generous white space
- Body text in Meno Display Light or Georgia
