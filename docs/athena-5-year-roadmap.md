# THE TWO ENGINES

Athena has **two growth engines** that must run in parallel:

| Engine | What It Does | Revenue Model |
|---|---|---|
| **RETAIL ROLL-UP** | Acquire existing adult retail stores, rebrand, optimize, cross-sell | Store revenue + margin expansion + real estate |
| **PLATFORM BUILD** | Creator storefronts, DTC, subscriptions, media, private label | Product margin + subscriptions + creator commissions + sponsorships |

The roll-up generates **cash flow and scale now**.  
The platform builds **defensibility and long-term value**.

| Pathway | What It Means |
