```
Something I've learned from acquiring four businesses in 18 months:

Due diligence spreadsheets won't tell you the most important thing about a company.

The culture will.

But if the culture is built on fear, secrecy, or ego — you're not acquiring a business. You're acquiring a problem.

Every acquisition we evaluate at Athena goes through a cultural review alongside the financial review.
```
