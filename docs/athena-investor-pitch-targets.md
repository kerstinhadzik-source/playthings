## PITCH ANGLE SUMMARY

When pitching any of these firms, lead with:

1. **Market size** — $34B global sexual wellness market growing 8% annually to $52B+ by 2028
2. **Structural gap** — The industry is underbanked, fragmented, and stigmatized. That's the opportunity, not the risk.
3. **Dual-engine model** — Retail roll-up generates cash flow NOW. Platform builds long-term defensibility.
4. **Proof** — 4 acquisitions closed. Quality earnings report done. Corporate structure built. Pipeline of 997+ targets.
5. **Female-led** — In a category that serves women, led by a woman. That matters for brand trust and for ESG-minded investors.
6. **Comps** — Lovehoney/WOW Tech merged at $1.2B. Playboy bought Lovers for $25M at ~5x EBITDA. Flynt bought it back for $13.5M (distressed). The multiples are there when the operator is right.
