## STEP 3: CONTENT PLAN — FIRST 2 WEEKS

### @kerstinhadzik — Instagram (Weeks 1–2)

Post 3–4x per week. Mix of formats.

#### Week 1: "Arrival" — Announce your presence without explaining everything

| Day | Format | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Photo + caption | Professional headshot. Caption: origin story in 3 sentences. "This is what I'm building. This is why." No hard sell. End with a question. |
| Day 2 | Story | BTS of your workspace or morning routine. Human, not curated. |
| Day 3 | Carousel | "5 things I believe about sexual wellness" — one belief per slide. Clean design. Warm palette. Last slide: "More coming. Follow along." |
| Day 5 | Reel (30–60s) | Direct-to-camera. "I'm building something no one's done before in this space. Here's the one-sentence version." Authentic, not scripted. |
| Day 7 | Story | Share something you're reading, watching, or thinking about. Build the "interested in her mind" factor. |

#### Week 2: "Perspective" — Establish your POV without selling

| Day | Format | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Day 8 | Text graphic + caption | A quote or belief from the woManifesto. Bold statement. Caption unpacks it. |
| Day 9 | Story | Quick poll or question box: "What's the one thing about sexual wellness nobody talks about?" (engagement driver) |
| Day 10 | Carousel | "Why I left [industry/path] to build this." Your professional credibility story — acquisitions, building companies, the shift to this space. |
| Day 12 | Reel | "The problem with the sexual wellness industry in 30 seconds." Hot take format. Direct. Confident. |
| Day 14 | Photo + caption | Something personal but professional. The intersection of who you are and what you're building. |

### @kerstinhadzik — TikTok (Weeks 1–2)

Post 3–4x per week. All video. 15–60 seconds.

**Content themes:**
- "Things no one tells women about ___"
- "I'm a CEO in the sexual wellness industry. Here's what that actually means."

## WHAT NOT TO DO

- Don't buy followers
- Don't use engagement pods
- Don't post and delete (the algorithm punishes this)
- Don't use 30 hashtags (3–5 relevant ones max)
- Don't announce everything you're going to do — show it being done
- Don't explain the corporate structure on social (nobody cares yet)
- Don't post inconsistently — it's better to post 2x/week reliably than 7x one week and nothing the next
- Don't make playthings "fun" — it's not fun. It's trustworthy. That's more powerful.
