## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Athena Enterprises is pursuing a **buying partnership** with Icon Brands to distribute their affordable portable product lines — primarily **The 9's** and the **Affordable Portables** collection — into smoke shops, head shops, and tobacco/vape retail stores across the United States.

### The Model: Business in a Box

This isn't traditional distribution. It's a **turnkey retail program** for smoke shop owners:

1. **FREE branded display case** — given to any retailer who places a first order at a trade show (or during a sales visit)
2. **Pre-loaded with a curated assortment** of 20–30 proven, impulse-priced Icon products
3. **Monthly subscription refill** — product automatically ships to restock what sold, no reordering hassle
4. **The smoke shop owner does nothing** except put the display near the register and collect money

The display is the razor. The subscription refill is the blade. The smoke shop gets a zero-risk, zero-effort new revenue stream. Athena gets locked-in recurring revenue.

### Why This Works

Smoke shops are rapidly evolving beyond tobacco and cannabis accessories. Retailers are looking for **high-margin impulse products** that drive basket size and foot traffic. Icon Brands' $5 wholesale / $9.99 MSRP price point is the perfect impulse buy — small, discreet, couples-friendly, and priced to move.

But the real unlock is **removing friction**. Smoke shop owners are busy. They don't want to learn a new product category, figure out what to order, or remember to restock. The Business in a Box model says: *"We'll handle all of that. You just give us counter space."*

### The Opportunity

- **71,000+ smoke shops** in the U.S. (and growing)
- **$8.2B U.S. adult product market** (2024), growing at 6.8% CAGR
- **Adult-smoke shop crossover** is an emerging distribution trend — distributors like Williams Trading and Full Circle Distribution are already proving the model
- Icon's value-priced products eliminate the barrier that has historically kept adult novelties out of non-adult retail: **price sensitivity and packaging stigma**
- **No one is offering a subscription refill model** for this category in smoke shops — first-mover advantage

### Athena's Role

Athena serves as a **distribution partner / regional buyer** — purchasing Icon products at wholesale and deploying them into smoke shop accounts through the Business in a Box subscription program, branded under **Smoke Show**.

## 3. THE BUSINESS IN A BOX MODEL

This is the core of the entire strategy. Everything else — the Icon partnership, the trade show presence, the sales operation — exists to get these boxes into smoke shops.

### What the Smoke Shop Owner Gets

| Component | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Branded Display Case** | Smoke Show-branded counter display or small floor unit, pre-loaded with product |
| **Cost to Retailer** | **FREE** with first order (at trade show or sales visit) |
| **Initial Product Load** | 25–60 units across 10–15 top-selling SKUs (varies by tier) |
| **Retail Price Range** | $9.99–$16.95 per product |
| **Retailer Margin** | 40–55% on every unit sold |
| **Subscription Refill** | Monthly auto-shipment to replace what sold |
| **Effort Required** | Zero. Put the display on the counter. Collect money. |

### How the Subscription Refill Works

```
MONTH 1 — ACTIVATION
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Retailer orders at trade show or during visit     │
│ → Receives FREE display case + initial product    │
│ → Display goes near the register                  │
│ → Products sell at impulse price point             │

MONTH 2+ — AUTOMATIC REFILL
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Athena ships monthly refill box                   │
│ → Standard refill: 25–60 units (bestselling mix)   │
│ → Retailer gets invoiced on shipment              │
│ → Retailer restocks display in 5 minutes          │
│ → Swap in seasonal or new products as needed      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

ONGOING — OPTIMIZATION
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Track what sells per location                     │
│ → Adjust refill mix based on velocity data        │
│ → Upgrade top performers to larger displays       │
│ → Introduce new product lines to proven accounts  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Why the Display Case Is the Key

The free display case isn't just a perk — it's the **lock-in mechanism**:

1. **It's a physical commitment.** Once it's on the counter, it stays. Retailers don't remove displays that are making money.
2. **It brands the space.** Every smoke shop with a Smoke Show display is advertising for you.
3. **It removes the "what do I stock?" problem.** The display tells the owner exactly what goes where.
4. **It creates switching cost.** If a competitor shows up, the retailer already has your display, your product, and your refill coming. Why switch?
5. **It's the trade show closer.** "Walk away today with a free display case loaded with product" is the strongest show offer in the building.

- Owner-operated
- 1 location
- Decision-maker is behind the counter
- Most common type in the U.S.

#### Tier 2 — Regional Chains (3–20 locations)
- Central buyer or owner group

- **Best for:** Standard/Premium display in all locations, single subscription billing

- Corporate purchasing
- Longer sales cycle, higher volume
- Examples: Wild Bill's, Smoker Friendly, various regional chains
- **Best for:** Premium displays, co-branded opportunities, exclusive SKUs

### Monthly Refill Box Operations

**Refill Packing Day** — One day per month (or weekly as volume grows):

1. Pull subscription list: which accounts, which tier, any custom swaps
2. Pack refill boxes (15–40 units per account depending on tier)
3. Include monthly insert card (what's new, seasonal highlight)
4. Ship via UPS/USPS or batch with local delivery route
5. Invoice automatically (Stripe, Square, or manual)
6. Track delivery confirmation
7. Flag any accounts that haven't reordered or have been skipping

### Fulfillment Methods

| Method | When | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Batch shipping (UPS/USPS)** | Most accounts — monthly refill day | $5–$12 per box |
| **In-person delivery** | Local accounts within 30–50 miles — build relationship | Mileage only |
| **Trade show pickup** | Initial display + product picked up at booth | $0 shipping |

### Subscription Management (Start Simple)

**Phase 1 (0–50 accounts):**
- Google Sheet or Airtable: account name, tier, ship date, payment status
- Square or Stripe for recurring billing
- Manual packing and shipping

**Phase 2 (50–150 accounts):**
- Shopify wholesale or simple subscription management tool
- Semi-automated billing
- Part-time packing help

**Phase 3 (150+ accounts):**
- Dedicated subscription management system
- Warehouse space or 3PL
- Full-time operations support

## 12. RISK FACTORS & MITIGATION

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscription churn** | Accounts cancel, MRR drops | Monitor churn monthly. Check in with accounts that skip. Offer seasonal variety. Keep it easy. |
| **Slow sell-through at some locations** | Refill boxes pile up, retailer cancels | Track velocity per account. Adjust refill mix for slow movers. Swap in different SKUs. Downgrade tier. |
| **Display cases not prominent enough** | Product doesn't get seen, doesn't sell | Provide placement guide. Visit accounts in person. Offer larger display for high-traffic spots. |
| **Icon pricing changes** | Margin compression | Lock in pricing tiers in writing; build toward volume commitments |
| **Retailer resistance ("we don't sell that")** | Slow account acquisition | "Free display, 50% margin, cancel anytime." Remove every objection. |
| **Regulatory / zoning** | Some municipalities restrict adult products in non-adult stores | Curate a "smoke shop safe" SKU list — rings, candles, couple-friendly items only |
| **Competition copies the model** | Other distributors launch their own box | Move fast. Lock accounts. Build Smoke Show brand loyalty. First mover wins in subscription. |
| **Cash flow timing** | Buying inventory + displays before subscription revenue catches up | Start with 25–30 displays. Reinvest first revenue. Use Net 30 from Icon. |
| **Trade show ROI** | Spend on booth but don't convert enough accounts | Set hard conversion goal (25+ signups per show). Pre-qualify leads. Follow up within 48 hours. |
