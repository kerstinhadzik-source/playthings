# PART I: CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW

## Goal

Position Erica Grisby and SLC Creation Lab as the go-to creator residency partner for luxury hotels, boutique restaurants, lifestyle brands, and tourism boards — using social media content as the proof of concept and the pitch all at once.

## The Big Idea

**"The content IS the pitch."**

Instead of cold-pitching brands with a PDF alone, the social media campaign creates a public portfolio of aspirational collab-style content that makes brands come to Erica — while the outreach materials (email, DM, PDF) close the deal.

Every post should make a hotel GM, marketing director, or brand manager think: *"I want that for my property."*

## Core Positioning

> **SLC Creation Lab — Luxury Content, Creator Residency Style**
>
> We don't just take photos at your property. We move in for 2–5 days, live the guest experience, and create a full library of editorial-quality content — photo, video, and story — that you own and we both share.
>
> No stock. No staged. No generic influencer selfies.
> Just real moments, elevated.

## Content Pillars (5 Pillars)

### 1. THE RESIDENCY — "What a Creator Residency Looks Like"
Show the process. This is the pillar that sells the service.

Content ideas:
- "72 hours at [Hotel Name] — here's what we created"
- Time-lapse of a suite-to-content transformation
- Before/after: iPhone snap vs. final delivered image
- "What I delivered vs. what the hotel expected" (exceed expectations)
- Carousel: "5 images from 1 stay — this is what a residency produces"

### 2. THE PORTFOLIO — "The Work Speaks"
Polished, final deliverables showcased beautifully.

Content ideas:
- Single stunning hero image with location tag + brand tag
- Carousel of 8–10 images from one property
- Reel: cinematic montage of a hotel walkthrough
- Grid-worthy architectural / interior / food shots
- "Shot on location at [Property]" — editorial style

### 3. THE PROCESS — "Behind the Lens"
Humanize the work. Show brands what it's like to work with you.

Content ideas:
- "How I plan a hotel shoot before I arrive" (mood board walkthrough)
- Gear rundown: "What I pack for a 3-day residency"
- "The shot list I send every property before check-in"
- BTS reel: setting up a restaurant flat-lay at 6am
- "What brands get wrong about content creators" (hot take)

### 4. THE LIFESTYLE — "I Live This"
Position Erica as someone who belongs in these spaces — not someone visiting them.

Content ideas:
- Candid luxury travel moments (not overly posed)
- "Morning routine at [Hotel]" — coffee, robe, balcony
- Restaurant content: food, ambiance, details most people miss
- City guides: "My favorite 3 spots in [City] for content"
- Fashion/styling moments that signal taste and credibility

### 5. THE PITCH — "Let's Work Together"
Direct calls to action for brands and properties.

Content ideas:
- "If your hotel needs content that actually converts — DM me"
- Testimonial graphics from past collabs
- "Here's what a 3-day creator residency includes" (carousel breakdown)
- "Now booking Q2/Q3 2026 residencies" announcement
- Link to the Luxury Content Collaboration PDF in bio

# PART VII: TARGET BRAND LIST — WHO TO PITCH

## Hotels & Resorts (Start Here)

### Boutique / Independent (Most Likely to Say Yes)
- New hotel openings in SLC, Park City, Denver, Scottsdale, LA, Nashville
- Boutique hotels with strong Instagram presence but inconsistent content
- Properties that just renovated or rebranded
- Hotels with <10K followers but beautiful spaces (underserved)
- Ski resorts and mountain lodges entering off-season (need content for next season)

### Hotel Groups to Target
- Kimpton Hotels
- Ace Hotel
- The LINE Hotels
- Graduate Hotels
- Auberge Resorts Collection
- Thompson Hotels
- 1 Hotels
- citizenM
- Small Luxury Hotels of the World (SLH)
- Design Hotels

### Tourism Boards
- Visit Salt Lake
- Visit Park City
- Visit Utah
- State tourism boards in surrounding states

## Restaurants
- High-end restaurants pre-opening or rebranding
- James Beard-nominated spots needing content refresh
- Restaurant groups (multiple locations = multiple residencies)

## Lifestyle Brands (For Integrated Collabs)
- Luggage/travel brands (Away, Monos, Calpak)
- Skincare/beauty brands that align with luxury travel
- Fashion brands that fit the editorial aesthetic
- Coffee/beverage brands (shot on location at hotels)
- Wellness brands (spa integrations)
