## 1. Business Model Overview

Techtonic LLC operates a **subscription-based direct-to-consumer (D2C) telehealth model** under the brand AdaptiveBioCore. Revenue is generated through a two-stage patient relationship: an initial paid consultation followed by an ongoing monthly treatment subscription.

```
Patient Discovers AdaptiveBioCore
        ↓
Completes Intake Questionnaire (Free)
        ↓
Pays for Consultation + Hormone Panel → $250 (one-time)
        ↓
Completes Lab Work → Telehealth Visit
        ↓
If Medically Appropriate → Prescription Issued
        ↓
Enrolls in Monthly Treatment Subscription → $199/month (recurring)
        ↓
Ongoing: Monthly Medication Delivery + Clinical Monitoring
        ↓
Periodic: Follow-Up Lab Panels → $150 (every 6–12 months)
```

## 2. Revenue Streams

### Stream 1: Initial Consultation Package — $250

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **What the patient gets** | Licensed physician consultation + comprehensive hormone panel (bloodwork) |
| **Price** | $250 (one-time) |
| **Payment timing** | Collected upfront before lab work is ordered |
| **Cost to deliver** | ~$80 (lab processing + provider time + platform costs) |
| **Gross margin** | ~68% |
| **Conversion source** | Digital advertising, organic search, referrals |

### Stream 2: Monthly Treatment Subscription — $199/month

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **What the patient gets** | Monthly medication supply (compounded and shipped), ongoing clinical monitoring, provider access |
| **Price** | $199/month |
| **Payment timing** | Recurring monthly charge via stored credit card |
| **Cost to deliver** | ~$71/month (pharmacy, provider, platform, processing) |
| **Gross margin** | ~64% |
| **Average retention** | 8–10 months |
| **Churn rate** | ~10%/month (conservative estimate) |

### Stream 3: Follow-Up Lab Panels — $150

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **What the patient gets** | Repeat bloodwork for treatment optimization and monitoring |
| **Price** | $150 |
| **Payment timing** | One-time, typically at 6 and 12 month marks |
| **Cost to deliver** | ~$50 |
| **Gross margin** | ~67% |
| **Frequency** | 1–2x per year per active patient |

## 3. Unit Economics — Single Patient Lifetime

### Customer Acquisition

| Metric | Value | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Ad Spend (at scale) | $15,000 | Budget allocation |
| Cost Per Click (avg.) | $4.50 | Google/Meta blended average for health keywords |
| Click-to-Lead Conversion | 10% | Landing page conversion rate |
| Leads Per Month | 333 | $15,000 ÷ $4.50 × 10% |
| Lead-to-Consultation Conversion | 10% | From lead to paying $250 |
| New Paying Patients/Month | ~33 | 333 leads × 10% |
| **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** | **~$120** | Based on: $15,000 ÷ 25 patients (conservative) |

> **Note:** CAC is calculated conservatively at ~$120 per acquired patient (factoring in ad spend ÷ converted patients, not just leads). As brand awareness grows and organic/referral channels mature, CAC is expected to decrease.

### Revenue Per Patient

| Period | Revenue | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Month 0 (Onboarding) | $250 | Initial consultation package |
| Months 1–8 (avg. retention) | $1,592 | $199 × 8 months |
| Follow-Up Labs (1x during lifetime) | $150 | At ~6 month mark |
| **Total Lifetime Value (LTV)** | **$1,992** | $250 + $1,592 + $150 |

### Cost Per Patient (Lifetime)

|---|---|---|

| **Total Lifetime Cost** | **$818** | |

### Profitability Per Patient

| Metric | Amount |
|---|---|

## 7. Key Takeaways for Lender

1. **Predictable Recurring Revenue:** The subscription model generates stable, predictable monthly cash flows once patients are onboarded — similar to SaaS business models that lenders are increasingly familiar with.

2. **Strong Unit Economics:** An LTV:CAC ratio of 16.6:1 is exceptional. The initial consultation fee ($250) covers the customer acquisition cost in the first transaction, meaning the business doesn't "go negative" to acquire customers.

3. **Scalable Without Proportional Cost Increases:** The API-driven technology stack means adding patients doesn't require proportional increases in staff or infrastructure. Marginal costs per patient are low and fixed.

4. **Multiple Monetization Points:** Revenue comes from three sources (consultations, subscriptions, follow-up labs), reducing dependence on any single stream.

5. **Market Tailwinds:** The telehealth and men's health markets are both in strong growth phases, with favorable regulatory and consumer behavior trends.
