## 1. Executive Summary

**Techtonic LLC**, operating under the brand **AdaptiveBioCore**, is a direct-to-consumer (D2C) telehealth company focused on health and hormone optimization. The company provides affordable, physician-supervised access to peptide therapies and testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) through a fully digital platform.

**The Problem:** Access to hormone optimization treatments is expensive, fragmented, and often unregulated. Traditional clinics charge $300–$500+ per consultation, require in-person visits, and lack transparency on pricing.

**The Solution:** AdaptiveBioCore provides an end-to-end digital experience — from initial consultation and bloodwork through ongoing prescription fulfillment — at a transparent, subscription-based price point significantly below traditional alternatives.

**Revenue Model:** Two-stage subscription
- Initial consultation + hormone panel: **$250**
- Monthly treatment subscription: **$199/month**

**Funding Request:** $200,000 SBA Express Loan for technology infrastructure, working capital, digital marketing, and professional services to launch and operate through profitability.

**Key Financial Projections (Year 1):**
- Projected Year 1 Revenue: **$583,550**
- Projected Year 1 Net Income: **$76,850**
- Break-even: **Month 5**
- Debt Service Coverage Ratio (Month 12): **6.8x**

## 3. Problem & Opportunity

### The Problem

1. **Cost Barrier:** Traditional hormone clinics charge $300–$500 for initial consultations, plus $200–$400/month for treatment, with additional lab fees.
2. **Access Barrier:** In-person clinic visits require geographic proximity, time off work, and scheduling friction.
3. **Quality & Safety Concerns:** A growing number of unregulated online sellers offer peptides and hormones without physician oversight, creating health risks.
4. **Fragmented Experience:** Patients must independently coordinate between doctors, labs, and pharmacies — a confusing and time-consuming process.

### The Opportunity

- The **global telehealth market** is projected to reach **$590 billion by 2030** (CAGR of 24.3%).
- The **testosterone replacement therapy market** is projected to reach **$2.2 billion by 2030**.
- The **peptide therapeutics market** is projected to reach **$78 billion by 2030**.
- Post-pandemic consumer acceptance of telehealth has created a permanent behavioral shift — **76% of patients** express willingness to use telehealth services.
- Regulatory tailwinds: Increasing state-level support for telehealth prescribing and interstate medical licensure compacts.

## 9. Operations & Technology

### Technology Stack ("Clinic Stack")

Techtonic leverages a modular, API-driven technology stack orchestrated by **Ola Health** as the central integrator:

| System | Function | Integration |
|---|---|---|
| **Website (AdaptiveBioCore.com)** | Patient discovery, marketing, CTA | Custom-built, Vite/JS |
| **Intake Platform** | Medical questionnaire, consent forms, lead capture | API webhook to Ola Health |
| **Ola Health (Integrator)** | Central workflow orchestration, patient routing, provider matching | Core API hub |
| **EHR System** | Electronic health records, e-prescribing, HIPAA-compliant charting | Integrated via Ola Health |
| **Telehealth Platform** | Secure video consultations with licensed providers | API-integrated |
| **Pharmacy Fulfillment** | Compounding pharmacy, medication dispensing, shipping | E-prescription API, tracking API |
| **Payment Gateway** | Subscription billing, one-time payments | Stripe / integrated via platform |
| **CRM / Email** | Patient communications, marketing automation | Integrated |

### Operational Workflow

1. **Patient onboards** via AdaptiveBioCore.com → redirected to intake platform
2. **Intake data** transmitted via API webhooks to Ola Health
3. **Ola Health** creates patient record, assigns provider, schedules consultation
4. **Payment processed** via integrated payment gateway
5. **Lab orders** issued and results routed to EHR
6. **Telehealth consultation** conducted via secure platform
7. **Prescription** e-prescribed to fulfillment pharmacy
8. **Pharmacy** compounds, packages, and ships medication with tracking
9. **Subscription billing** initiated for recurring monthly charges

## 10. Regulatory & Compliance

### Key Regulatory Considerations

| Area | Requirement | Techtonic's Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **HIPAA** | Protected Health Information (PHI) security | All platform partners are HIPAA-compliant; BAAs in place |
| **Telehealth Licensing** | Providers must be licensed in patient's state | Multi-state licensed providers via platform partnerships |
| **E-Prescribing** | DEA and state-level requirements for controlled substances | Compliant e-prescribing through EHR integration |
| **Pharmacy Regulations** | State board of pharmacy requirements | Licensed compounding pharmacy partners |
| **Advertising** | FTC and FDA guidelines for health claims | Legal review of all marketing materials |
| **Payment Compliance** | PCI-DSS for cardholder data | Payment processing via PCI-compliant gateway |
| **Interstate Medical Licensure Compact** | Multi-state practice facilitation | Leverage IMLC for provider coverage expansion |

### Risk Mitigation

- All clinical decisions made by licensed, credentialed physicians
- Legal counsel specializing in telehealth regulatory compliance retained
- Ongoing compliance monitoring and policy updates
- Professional liability insurance for clinical operations
- HIPAA training for all personnel with PHI access
