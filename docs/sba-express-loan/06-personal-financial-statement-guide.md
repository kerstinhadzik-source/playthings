### Section 1: Assets (What You OWN)

List everything you own and its current fair market value:

| Asset Category | What to Include | Tips |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash on Hand & in Banks** | All checking, savings, money market accounts | Use current balances; get statements dated within 30 days |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Don't leave fields blank** — Write "$0" or "N/A" if not applicable
2. **Don't undervalue assets** — Use fair market value, not purchase price
3. **Don't omit liabilities** — The SBA and lender WILL pull your credit report; undisclosed debts are a red flag
4. **Don't forget retirement accounts** — These count as assets even if you can't access them easily
5. **Don't include business assets** — Form 413 is PERSONAL only
6. **Sign and date the form** — Unsigned forms will be rejected
7. **Use a recent date** — The form should be dated within 90 days of application

## Additional Notes

- The lender may also request a **personal credit report authorization** — sign it; they need to verify
- If married and spouse co-owns property, the spouse may also need to complete Form 413 (check with lender)
- Keep a copy of the completed form for your records
- All information is confidential and protected under SBA privacy policies
