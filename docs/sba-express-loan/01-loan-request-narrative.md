The company delivers affordable access to peptide-based therapies and testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) by connecting patients with licensed physicians through a fully digital platform.

Our business addresses a significant market gap: **access to health and hormone optimization services is prohibitively expensive and difficult to navigate** for most adults. By leveraging an API-driven technology stack with integrated telehealth, electronic health records, and pharmacy fulfillment, Techtonic provides a compliant, scalable, and patient-friendly experience at a fraction of the cost of traditional clinics.

## 3. Revenue Model & Ability to Repay

Techtonic generates revenue through a **two-stage subscription model**:

- **Stage 1 — Initial Consultation:** Each patient pays **$250** for a doctor visit and hormone panel (bloodwork).
- **Stage 2 — Monthly Subscription:** Patients approved for treatment pay a **recurring monthly fee of $199/month** for medication, ongoing clinical monitoring, and provider access.

**Key Unit Economics:**
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): ~$120 (based on digital advertising spend)
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU): $199/month
- Lifetime Value (LTV) at 8-month avg. retention: ~$1,842
- LTV:CAC Ratio: **15.4:1**

**Projected Loan Repayment:**
Based on conservative financial projections (see attached 12-month pro forma), the business is projected to achieve **monthly positive cash flow by Month 5** and generate sufficient net income to comfortably service the loan obligation within the standard SBA Express Loan term.

At a 10-year term with an estimated interest rate of Prime + 2.75%, the approximate monthly payment is **$2,283**. The business is projected to generate monthly net income exceeding **$15,000 by Month 8**, providing a **debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) well above the 1.25x** typically required by SBA lenders.
