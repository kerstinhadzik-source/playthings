## What is an SBA Express Loan?

- **Maximum amount:** $500,000
- **SBA guarantee:** 50% (for loans up to $500,000)
- **SBA turnaround time:** 36 hours (compared to 5–10 business days for standard 7(a))
- **Term:** Up to 10 years for working capital; up to 25 years for real estate
- **Interest rate:** Negotiable; maximum Prime + 4.5% (for loans $50K–$250K) or Prime + 6.5% (for loans $250K–$500K)
- **Lender uses own forms:** The lender may have their own application in addition to SBA forms
- **Collateral:** Lender follows their existing collateral policy; no SBA-specific collateral requirements for Express loans up to $25K. For loans >$25K, lender may require collateral per their policy.
- **Personal guarantee:** Required for all owners with 20%+ ownership

- [ ] **SBA Form 413** — Completed and signed by Madeline Marschak
  - Download: https://www.sba.gov/document/sba-form-413-personal-financial-statement

## Pre-Application Preparation Checklist

Before approaching a lender, make sure you have:

- [ ] **Identified SBA Preferred Lenders in your area** — These lenders have delegated authority to approve SBA loans without sending to SBA for review, which speeds up the process
  - Use SBA Lender Match: https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans/lender-match
  - Local banks, credit unions, and CDFIs often participate
- [ ] **Checked your personal credit score** — Pull your free report at annualcreditreport.com
  - Target: 680+ FICO (some lenders accept 650+)
  - Dispute any errors before applying
- [ ] **Opened a business bank account** (if not already done)
- [ ] **Obtained an EIN** from the IRS (if not already done)
- [ ] **Registered the business** in all required states
- [ ] **Consulted with a SCORE mentor or SBA SBDC counselor** — Free mentoring and application assistance
  - SCORE: https://www.score.org
  - SBDC: https://americassbdc.org

## Tips for a Strong Application

1. **Be organized** — Present all documents in a professional, clearly labeled binder or digital folder
2. **Be honest** — Don't inflate numbers; the lender will verify everything
3. **Show you've done your homework** — The comprehensive business plan and financial projections demonstrate seriousness
4. **Explain the "why"** — The cover letter should tell a compelling story about why this business will succeed
5. **Address weaknesses proactively** — If you're a startup with no revenue history, acknowledge it and point to market validation, your business model, and your projections
6. **Practice your pitch** — You may need to present to a loan committee. Know your numbers cold.
7. **Leverage free resources** — SCORE mentors and SBDC counselors can review your application before you submit
8. **Apply to multiple lenders** — Each lender has different appetites and criteria. Don't put all eggs in one basket.
9. **Keep liquid reserves** — Lenders want to see that you have some personal savings / liquidity as a buffer
10. **Follow up** — After submitting, follow up weekly. Show enthusiasm and responsiveness.
