# SMOKE SHOW — BRANDING CONCEPTS
## Visual Identity & Display Design for Smoke Shop Channel

**Date:** February 2026  
**Brand:** Smoke Show (under Athena Enterprises)  
**Purpose:** Sell Icon Brands' affordable portables into smoke shops via the Business in a Box subscription model  
**Key Constraint:** Must grab attention in a dark, cluttered, neon-lit smoke shop environment  

---

## THE ENVIRONMENT YOU'RE DESIGNING FOR

Before picking colors and fonts, understand where this display lives:

- **Lighting:** Dim overhead, bright LED product spotlights, neon signs
- **Color palette in the room:** Black, dark wood, chrome, glass, random neon (blue, green, red from vape displays)
- **Clutter level:** HIGH — glass cases full of pipes, vape walls, rolling papers, lighters, CBD products, kratom, snacks
- **Counter space:** Precious. Usually cluttered with tip jars, business cards, vape testers
- **Customer dwell time at register:** 30–90 seconds — your display has to grab attention FAST
- **Vibe:** Urban, male-skewing, 21–45, comfortable with "vice" products

**The display needs to:**
1. Be visible from 10+ feet away (across the shop)
2. Look like it belongs in the shop (not sterile or clinical)
3. Stand out from everything else (not another vape display)
4. Communicate "adult/couples products" without being explicit
5. Make the products look premium even at a $10–$15 price point
6. Be compact enough for counter or small shelf placement

---

## DIRECTION 1: NEON HEAT (RECOMMENDED)

### The Vibe
Vegas marquee meets late-night energy. Hot pink neon glow against matte black. The display literally lights up — you can't miss it. It says "something exciting is here" without saying a word.

### Why This Direction Wins
- **Connects to Athena's DNA** — your "Push to Play" campaign is already hot pink neon. This is brand-consistent across the portfolio.
- **LED-lit displays dominate smoke shops** — every vape brand uses LED. This speaks the visual language of the environment.
- **Hot pink is the disruptor** — smoke shops are dominated by blue, green, and white LEDs. Hot pink is the color NO ONE is using. It's a beacon.
- **"Smoke Show" as a name demands this treatment** — it's flashy, confident, attention-grabbing. The branding should match.

### Color Palette

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Hot Pink (Neon)** | #FF1493 or #FF007F | Logo, LED glow, accents, display lighting |
| **Matte Black** | #1A1A1A | Display body, backgrounds, packaging |
| **Soft White / Cream** | #F5F0EB | Secondary text, product labels |
| **Electric Pink Glow** | #FF69B4 | LED diffused glow, halo effect behind display |

### Logo Concept

**Primary Mark:**  
"SMOKE SHOW" in a bold, clean sans-serif (think Montserrat Heavy, Bebas Neue, or custom) — all caps, wide tracking. Not script, not cursive. Bold and legible from across the room.

**Alternate/Secondary:**  
A neon-sign version — "Smoke Show" in a neon script feel, as if it were a real bent-tube neon sign. Used for social media, print materials, trade show signage.

**Icon/Logomark:**  
A simple flame or smoke wisp integrated into the "S" — subtle, not heavy-handed. Or a small lips/kiss mark as a wink to the adult category.

### Display Case Design

**Structure:**
- **Material:** Clear acrylic body with matte black base and header
- **Size:** ~14" wide x 10" deep x 18" tall (Starter), larger for Standard/Premium
- **Tiers:** 2–3 angled shelves so products face forward and are easy to grab
- **LED strip:** Hot pink LED strip along the base and/or header — plugs into USB (cashiers always have a USB port nearby)
- **Header panel:** Matte black with "SMOKE SHOW" logo in hot pink (printed, vinyl, or edge-lit acrylic)

**What it looks like in the shop:**
Imagine walking into a smoke shop. Everything is glass and chrome and vape displays with blue/green LEDs. Near the register, there's a compact black display glowing hot pink. "SMOKE SHOW" across the top. Inside: colorful, playful products — bunny vibes, jeweled plugs, sensual candles, couples rings. It's the only thing in the store that looks like that. You walk toward it.

**Key Details:**
- Products face outward (not spine-out like books)
- Price tags visible: "$9.99" / "$14.99" — impulse range
- Small sign at bottom: "Ask about our monthly subscription" or QR code
- Back of display: Smoke Show logo + "smokeshowbox.com" (or chosen domain)

### Packaging / Product Presentation

The products are Icon Brands packaging (you don't control that). But you add:
- **Smoke Show sticker** on the front of each product (small, branded, "as seen in Smoke Show")
- **Or** a Smoke Show hang tag / sleeve that wraps around Icon packaging
- This brands the products as part of the Smoke Show collection, even though they're made by Icon

### Trade Show Booth

**Champs Austin Booth Concept:**
- **Backdrop:** Large matte black banner with "SMOKE SHOW" in hot pink neon (real LED neon or printed neon effect)
- **Table:** Black tablecloth, 3–5 loaded display cases across the front (showing different tiers)
- **Lighting:** Pink LED strips under the table, behind the backdrop
- **Products:** Out of packaging on risers so people can touch and see them
- **Signage:** "FREE DISPLAY CASE — ORDER TODAY" in large text
- **Signup station:** iPad on a stand or clipboard with subscription forms
- **Business cards:** Matte black with hot pink foil "SMOKE SHOW" + QR code
- **Giveaway:** Branded lighter or sticker with every signup (cheap, memorable, useful in smoke shop context)
- **Vibe:** The booth should feel like walking into a VIP section — dark, glowing, different from everything else at the show

## DIRECTION 2: BLACK & GOLD (Premium Alternative)

### The Vibe
High-end cigar lounge. Luxury positioning at a value price. Makes $10 products feel like $40. Matte black + gold foil. Classic, sophisticated, timeless.

### Color Palette

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Matte Black** | #1A1A1A | Display body, backgrounds |
| **Gold Foil** | #C9A84C | Logo, accents, trim |
| **Cream** | #F5F0EB | Secondary text |
| **Warm White LED** | — | Display lighting |

### Logo
"SMOKE SHOW" in an art deco or elegant serif typeface. Gold foil on black. Feels like a premium spirits brand or a high-end fragrance.

### Display Case
- Matte black acrylic with gold edge trim
- Warm white LED interior lighting (not colored)
- Gold foil logo on header panel
- Velvet or felt-lined shelves (makes products feel premium)

### Why You Might Choose This
- If you want to position Smoke Show as "elevated" rather than "fun"
- Appeals to an older demographic (30+)
- Looks great at Champs and industry events
- Premium feel could justify higher price points in the future

### Why It's Not #1
- Doesn't pop as hard in a chaotic smoke shop
- Gold can feel generic (vape brands already use black + gold)
- Doesn't match Athena's existing "Push to Play" hot pink energy
- Less Instagram-friendly / less shareable

## DIRECTION 3: FLAME GRADIENT (Bold Alternative)

### The Vibe
Streetwear meets pop art. A hot gradient (pink → orange → red, like fire) on white or black. Bold, loud, impossible to ignore. Young energy.

### Color Palette

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Hot Pink** | #FF1493 | Gradient start |
| **Orange** | #FF6B35 | Gradient middle |
| **Red/Fire** | #FF2400 | Gradient end |
| **White or Black** | — | Background alternating |

### Logo
"SMOKE SHOW" in chunky block letters filled with the flame gradient. Or white/black text with a gradient underline/accent.

### Display Case
- White acrylic body (stands out in dark shops by contrast)
- Flame gradient header panel
- RGB LED strip that cycles warm colors (pink → orange → red)
- Bold, graphic, looks like a piece of street art on the counter

### Why You Might Choose This
- Maximum visual impact — loudest option
- Great for younger demographics (21–30)
- Instagram / TikTok ready
- The gradient is eye-catching and unique

### Why It's Not #1
- Might feel too casual or "cheap" at trade shows
- Harder to reproduce consistently across materials
- Doesn't connect to Athena's existing brand palette as cleanly
- Could clash with Icon's product packaging (which is already colorful)

## RECOMMENDATION: GO WITH DIRECTION 1 — NEON HEAT

### Here's Why

| Factor | Neon Heat (Pink/Black) | Black & Gold | Flame Gradient |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visibility in smoke shop** | Highest — LED pink glow is a beacon | Medium — elegant but doesn't pop | High — bright but can clash |
| **Brand consistency (Athena)** | Perfect — matches Push to Play | Disconnected | Partial overlap |
| **Trade show impact** | Dominant — dark booth, neon glow | Premium but quiet | Loud but unfocused |
| **Instagram / social** | Very shareable — neon always photographs well | Decent | Very shareable |
| **Reproductibility** | Simple — two colors, one LED color | Simple | Complex — gradient printing |
| **Cost of displays** | Moderate — standard acrylic + pink LED | Higher — gold foil, velvet | Moderate — RGB LEDs |
| **Emotional response** | Excitement, curiosity, fun | Sophistication, trust | Energy, youth |
| **Matches "Smoke Show" name** | Yes — flashy, hot, attention | Partially | Yes — fiery |

### The Brand System at a Glance

```
BRAND NAME:     SMOKE SHOW
TAGLINE:        "Light It Up" or "Turn Heads" or "Counter Culture"
COLORS:         Hot pink neon (#FF007F) + Matte black (#1A1A1A)
TYPOGRAPHY:     Bold sans-serif, all caps, wide tracking
LOGO:           "SMOKE SHOW" wordmark + optional flame/smoke icon
LED COLOR:      Hot pink (USB-powered strip)
DISPLAY:        Clear acrylic + matte black base + pink LED
DOMAIN:         smokeshowbox.com (or chosen alternative)
SOCIAL:         @smokeshowbox (or chosen handle)
```

## WHAT THE RETAILER SEES — THE FULL EXPERIENCE

### Step 1: The Trade Show or Sales Visit
- Smoke Show booth / sales rep arrives with a glowing pink display
- Products are loaded, visible, touchable
- It looks like nothing else in the room

### Step 2: The Display Arrives at the Shop
- Matte black box arrives: "SMOKE SHOW" in hot pink
- Inside: display case, products pre-loaded, welcome card, USB LED plug
- Retailer sets it on the counter, plugs in the LED, done

### Step 3: In the Shop
- The display glows pink near the register
- Customers notice it — "What's that?"
- They pick up a product: $12.99. "Sure, I'll grab this."
- The display does the selling. No pitch from the cashier needed.

### Step 4: Monthly Refill Box
- Hot pink shipping box or mailer arrives each month
- "SMOKE SHOW" branding on the outside
- Inside: 15–25 products + a monthly insert card
- Retailer restocks the display in 5 minutes

### Step 5: The Display Stays
- It's making money. It looks great. It draws attention.
- Why would they remove it?
