# Athena Master Playbook v2.0  
## The Sexual Wellness Platform Blueprint

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Athena is a vertically integrated, creator-first sexual wellness platform designed to replace outdated MLM and fragmented retail models with scalable, ethical, and data-driven infrastructure.

Athena combines:
- Commerce  
- Media  
- Community  
- Education  
- Private Label Manufacturing  
- Creator Enablement  

Into one defensible ecosystem.

---

## 2. Vision & Mission

### Vision
To become the leading female-led sexual wellness platform in North America.

### Mission
Empower women to own their voice, income, and communities through modern sexual wellness commerce.

### Core Beliefs
- Ownership beats dependency  
- Education builds trust  
- Community creates loyalty  
- Infrastructure enables scale  
- Ethics drive longevity
