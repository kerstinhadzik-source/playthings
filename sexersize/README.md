---

## Quick Start

This is a static HTML/CSS/JS site. To preview locally:

1. Open `index.html` in a browser, OR
2. Use a local dev server:
   ```bash
   # If you have Node.js:
   npx serve .

   # Or Python:
   python -m http.server 8000
   ```

## Deployment

This site is ready to deploy to any static hosting:

- **Netlify**: Drag & drop the `sexersize` folder, or connect a GitHub repo
- **Vercel**: `npx vercel` from this directory
- **GitHub Pages**: Push to a repo and enable Pages in settings

## Social Media Strategy

### Phase 1: Build the Audience (Months 1-3)

**Primary Platforms:** Instagram + TikTok
**Secondary:** Threads, X/Twitter

#### Content Pillars

1. **Confessions** (40% of content)
   - Anonymous funny sex stories submitted by followers
   - "Read this and tell me it hasn't happened to you"
   - Format: Text-on-graphic using brand colors, carousel posts

2. **Real Talk** (25% of content)
   - Honest discussions about sex in your 40s
   - Menopause & libido, body changes, relationship evolution
   - Format: Reels/TikToks (talking head or voiceover with illustrations)

3. **Reviews & Recs** (15% of content)
   - Product spotlights using the SVG toy illustrations
   - "Your friend who tells you what to buy" energy
   - Format: Carousel posts, short-form video

4. **Memes & Humor** (20% of content)
   - Relatable 40s sex humor
   - Use the retro 80s aesthetic for branded memes
   - Format: Single image posts, Stories

#### Posting Cadence

| Platform | Frequency | Best Times |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| Instagram Feed | 4-5x/week | Tues-Fri, 11am-1pm or 7-9pm |
| Instagram Stories | Daily | Throughout the day |
| Instagram Reels | 3-4x/week | Wed & Fri evenings |
| TikTok | 5-7x/week | 7-9pm weekdays, 10am-12pm weekends |
| Threads | 3-4x/week | Mirror Instagram timing |
| X/Twitter | 2-3x/week | Lunchtime & evenings |

#### Growth Tactics

- **Story submissions**: Create a Google Form or Typeform for anonymous story submissions. Promote on every platform. User-generated content = free content + community building.
- **Hashtag strategy**: #SexAfter40 #FortyAndFrisky #SexerSize #WomenOver40 #SexPositive #LetsTalkAboutSex
- **Engagement**: Reply to EVERY comment. Ask questions. Create polls in Stories. Be the fun friend, not a brand.
- **Collaborations**: Partner with other women-focused humor accounts, sex educators, and relationship coaches.
- **Newsletter cross-promote**: Tease stories on social, full versions in the newsletter. Drives email signups.

### Phase 2: Monetize & Pivot to Ecomm (Months 4-6+)

Once you hit ~5-10K engaged followers:

1. **Affiliate links** for products mentioned in reviews
2. **Sponsored content** from sex-positive brands
3. **Launch a Shopify store** with curated products
4. Use the 100+ SVG toy illustrations as product category art
5. **Branded merch** — "Forty & Frisky" tees, tote bags, etc.
6. **Subscription content** — Premium story collections, guides
